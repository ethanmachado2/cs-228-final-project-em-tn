# Task 5 findings (rendered into summary.md by scripts/characterize.py)

- pyfing 0.7.3 SBMEX returned no minutiae at all under OpenCV 5.0.0. `Sbmex._encode_neighborhood` calls `cv.filter2D` with a uint8 kernel, and OpenCV 5 applies the wrong taps (a horizontal line encodes as 64+4 instead of 128+8), so ridge following stops at once and every candidate is dropped. `fpv.extract` uses a pure-numpy encoding that is identical to the float-kernel `filter2D` result. All numbers below use that fix.
- With the pre-study attempt semantics (pyfing told dpi = 500 × upscale, the `dpi_*` chains), SBMEX finds a median of 0–1 deduplicated minutiae, and only 0.5% of samples reach 12 on any attempt. Lowering `minimum_background_distance` to 4, 6 or 8 at 1x does not help (0.5%), and neither does the 2x dpi upscale (0%). pyfing resamples back to 500 dpi internally, which undoes the upscale. SOCOFing ridges are about 3–6 px apart, against about 9 px at a true 500 ppi.
- Resampling 3x and treating the result as 500 ppi (new attempt field `resample`) gives median deduplicated counts of 32 (traditional), 24 (deep) and 29 (deep + CLAHE, MBD 4). quality_after has a median of 57–61 at the 3x NFIQ 2 upscale. A 2x resample is not enough: 55–56% of samples reach 12.
- Decision: attempts = traditional, deep, and deep + CLAHE + MBD 4, each with `resample: 3.0`. At minutiae_min 12, cumulative acquisition is 99.9% / 100% / 100%. Attempt 3 is the best deep third attempt at a stricter 20-minutiae floor (99.0% cumulative vs 98.4% for MBD 6–10).
- Decision: `minutiae_min` stays 12. The cumulative rate is still 100% at 15 and 99.0% at 20, so Task 12 can sweep 10–20 without FTA concerns.
- NFIQ 2 pre-scoring upscale: at 3x the raw median is 41 (p10 19); at 2x it is 25 (p10 12). No sample is unscorable at either. quality_after medians are 57–61 at 3x vs 35–38 at 2x. 3x spreads the scores and keeps the gate away from the floor, so `quality.nfiq2_upscale` stays 3.0.
- Decision: `nfiq2_min` stays 5 on the 3x scale. It passes 99.1% of samples, rejecting only raw scores 0–4, which are concentrated in a few fingers (for example 179 L middle at every level). A cutoff of 10 would reject 2.8% overall and 5.0% of Altered-Hard. End-to-end acquisition with the gate is 99.1%. Task 12 re-sweeps the cutoff against FTE/FTA and matching accuracy.
