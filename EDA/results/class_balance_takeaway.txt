Original images constitute 10.9% of the retained training images (4,180), while altered images collectively constitute 89.1% (34,323). The unequal category counts motivate reporting verification results by alteration category alongside overall results. These are image-category counts, not genuine/impostor or live/spoof labels. Genuine/impostor balance will be defined when verification pairs are generated within each split; a controlled impostor-sampling ratio can then be chosen and documented. No oversampling or pair generation is performed by this analysis.
